Behind
Leaf AI

A deep-learning plant disease intelligence system built with MobileNetV2, Flask, and Gemini AI — designed to assist farmers, researchers, and plant enthusiasts.

// What is LeafAI

LeafAI is a modern web application that identifies plant diseases from leaf images using a convolutional neural network trained on the PlantVillage dataset. Upload any leaf photo and receive an instant diagnosis — along with actionable treatment advice and a 7-day recovery routine.

When model confidence drops below the threshold, Gemini AI seamlessly steps in as a fallback, ensuring reliable results even on ambiguous or unusual inputs. An integrated chatbot lets you ask follow-up questions about the diagnosis in plain language.

// Features
🔬
CNN Disease Detection
MobileNetV2 trained on 38+ disease classes from PlantVillage dataset.

Personalised recovery plans tailored to each detected disease.
